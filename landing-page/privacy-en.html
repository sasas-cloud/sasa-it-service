2. General

The protection of your personal data is a central concern for us. We process personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable national data protection regulations. This privacy policy describes which data we collect, for what purpose, and how we protect it.

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Data You Provide Directly

  • Communication data: When you contact us via email (info@sasa-it.de) or phone, we process the information you provide (name, email address, phone number, request content) to handle your request.
  • Contact data: When you submit inquiries through our website or in the context of contractual relationships, we collect necessary contact details (name, company name, address, email, phone number).

3.2 Automatically Collected Data

  • Server log files: Each time our website is accessed, our server automatically records information that can be attributed to the website communication (IP address, date and time of access, browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, requested files). These data are stored for ensuring the stable operation of the website, for website optimization, and for protecting the security of our IT systems.
  • Cookies: We use cookies on our website. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They serve to make the website more user-friendly (e.g., storing preferences). You can adjust your cookie settings at any time in your browser settings.

3.3 Analytics Tools

We do not use any external analytics services on our website. If we were to use Matomo or similar privacy-friendly tools in the future, we would name them in this policy and obtain consent where required by law.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Performance of contract and pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): To handle your request or fulfill an existing contractual relationship.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): For optimizing our website, ensuring the secure operation of our IT systems, and defending against attacks.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Where we obtain your explicit consent (e.g., for newsletters or certain cookies).
  • Legal obligations: Where we are required to comply with legal provisions (e.g., commercial register, tax, or accounting requirements).

5. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not generally disclose your personal data to third parties. Disclosure occurs only if:

  • a legal obligation exists (e.g., official orders),
  • this is necessary to fulfill contractual obligations with you, or
  • you have given explicit consent.

When we engage external service providers (e.g., hosting, email dispatch), we ensure that data processing is governed by contract and that the providers implement the required technical and organizational measures.

6. Storage Duration

We store your personal data only as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as required by legal retention periods. Once the purpose ceases or the retention period expires, data are routinely deleted or anonymized, unless legal retention obligations conflict with this.

7. Your Rights as Data Subject

You have the following rights against us:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR): You can request information at any time about the extent and purpose of the processing of your data.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): You can have your data corrected at any time.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): You can request the deletion of your data, unless legal retention obligations conflict with this.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR): Under certain conditions, you can request a restriction of processing.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): You have the right to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format or to have it transmitted to another controller.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR): You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77 GDPR): You have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority for data protection. The supervisory authority competent for us is the federal state in which our headquarters is located. A list of supervisory authorities with addresses is available online at www.bfdi.bund.de.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at info@sasa-it.de.

8. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. These include, among others, access controls, encryption, regular security checks, and training of our employees.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy to adapt it to current legal requirements or our changed services. The current privacy policy is always available on our website.

10. Contact
